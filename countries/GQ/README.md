# Equatorial Guinea — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `GQ` / `GNQ`  
**Country calling code:** +240  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XXX XXXX
International: +240 XX XXX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (3J XXX XXXX; 9 = Bioko, 8 = Litoral/Annobón, 7 = mainland) | `33 XXX XXXX` | `+240 33 XXX XXXX` |
| Mobile — GETESA, HiTs G.E. (2J, 5J) | `22 XXX XXXX` | `+240 22 XXX XXXX` |
| Freephone (80X) / premium (90X) | `80X XXX XXX` | `+240 80X XXX XXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+240333091234` | `+240 33 309 1234` | `33 309 1234` | Malabo, Bioko Island — fixed numbers begin with 3 |
| mobile | `+240222123456` | `+240 22 212 3456` | `22 212 3456` | Mobile — numbers beginning 2 or 5 |

## Notes

- Fixed numbers begin with 3 and mobile numbers with 2 or 5.
- Within the fixed plan a geographic digit identifies the region: 9 Bioko Island, 8 Litoral and Annobón, 7 Centro-Sur, Kié-Ntem and Wele-Nzás.
