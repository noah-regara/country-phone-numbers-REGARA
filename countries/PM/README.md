# Saint Pierre and Miquelon — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `PM` / `SPM`  
**Country calling code:** +508  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 6 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX
International: +508 XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (41, 55) | `41 XX XX` | `+508 41 XX XX` |
| Mobile (55) | `55 XX XX` | `+508 55 XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+508412345` | `+508 41 23 45` | `41 23 45` | Saint-Pierre — fixed lines in the 41 range |
| mobile | `+508551234` | `+508 55 12 34` | `55 12 34` | Mobile — the 55 range |

## Notes

- Saint Pierre and Miquelon keeps a short 6-digit plan rather than the 9-digit format used by the other French overseas territories.
- Numbers are written in pairs in the French style.
