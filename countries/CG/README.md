# Congo — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `CG` / `COG`  
**Country calling code:** +242  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      YY ZZ XXXXX
International: +242 YY ZZ XXXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line — Brazzaville (2228) | `22 28 XXXXX` | `+242 22 28 XXXXX` |
| Mobile — MTN Congo (06), Airtel (05) | `06 XX XXXXX` | `+242 06 XX XXXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+242222812345` | `+242 22 28 12345` | `22 28 12345` | Brazzaville — the 2228 fixed block serves the capital |
| mobile | `+242061234567` | `+242 06 123 4567` | `06 123 4567` | Mobile — 06 is an MTN Congo range |

## Notes

- The Republic of the Congo (+242) is distinct from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (+243) — the two are frequently confused.
- NSNs are 9 digits; mobile numbers keep their leading 0 when dialled internationally.
