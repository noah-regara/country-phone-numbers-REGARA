# Guatemala — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `GT` / `GTM`  
**Country calling code:** +502  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXXX-XXXX
International: +502 XXXX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guatemala City fixed (2, 6) | `2XXX-XXXX` | `+502 2XXX XXXX` |
| Provincial fixed (7) | `7XXX-XXXX` | `+502 7XXX XXXX` |
| Mobile (3, 4, 5) | `5XXX-XXXX` | `+502 5XXX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+50222345678` | `+502 2234 5678` | `2234-5678` | Guatemala City — leading 2 (also 6) denotes a capital landline |
| mobile | `+50251234567` | `+502 5123 4567` | `5123-4567` | Mobile — 3, 4 and 5 are mobile prefixes |
| fixed-line | `+50271234567` | `+502 7123 4567` | `7123-4567` | Provincial landline — leading 7 |

## Notes

- No area codes or trunk prefix; the first digit classifies the service (2/6 capital fixed, 7 provincial fixed, 3/4/5 mobile).
- The three digits after the first identify the service provider.
- 1-800 gives international toll-free access and 1-801 domestic toll-free.
