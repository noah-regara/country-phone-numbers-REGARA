# Senegal — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `SN` / `SEN`  
**Country calling code:** +221  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XXX XX XX
International: +221 XX XXX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (33) | `33 XXX XX XX` | `+221 33 XXX XX XX` |
| Mobile — Orange (77), Free (76), Expresso (70) | `77 XXX XX XX` | `+221 77 XXX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+221338123456` | `+221 33 812 34 56` | `33 812 34 56` | Dakar — 33 is the fixed-line prefix, 8 the Dakar area digit |
| mobile | `+221775123456` | `+221 77 512 34 56` | `77 512 34 56` | Mobile — 77 is an Orange (Sonatel) range |
| mobile | `+221761234567` | `+221 76 123 45 67` | `76 123 45 67` | Mobile — 76 range |

## Notes

- Closed 9-digit plan with no trunk prefix; the leading two digits identify service and operator rather than geography.
- 33 is fixed-line nationally; mobile ranges are 70, 75, 76, 77 and 78.
