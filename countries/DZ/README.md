# Algeria — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `DZ` / `DZA`  
**Country calling code:** +213  
**Trunk prefix:** `0`  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 or 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      0AA XX XX XX (landline) / 0AXX XX XX XX (mobile)
International: +213 AA XX XX XX (trunk 0 dropped)
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Algiers (021, 023) | `021 XX XX XX` | `+213 21 XX XX XX` |
| Other wilayas (2-digit area codes) | `0XX XX XX XX` | `+213 XX XX XX XX` |
| Mobile — Ooredoo (05), Mobilis (06), Djezzy (07) | `05XX XX XX XX` | `+213 5XX XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+21321123456` | `+213 21 12 34 56` | `021 12 34 56` | Algiers — area codes 021 and 023 |
| mobile | `+213550123456` | `+213 550 12 34 56` | `0550 12 34 56` | Mobile — 05 denotes Ooredoo |
| mobile | `+213660123456` | `+213 660 12 34 56` | `0660 12 34 56` | Mobile — 06 denotes Mobilis |

## Notes

- Landline NSNs are 8 digits and mobile NSNs 9, so length distinguishes the two.
- The digit after the trunk 0 identifies the mobile operator: 5 Ooredoo, 6 Mobilis, 7 Djezzy.
- Numbers are conventionally written in pairs in the French style.
