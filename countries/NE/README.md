# Niger — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `NE` / `NER`  
**Country calling code:** +227  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX XX (AB identifies service/network)
International: +227 XX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line — Niamey (20 20) | `20 XX XX XX` | `+227 20 XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (9X, 8X) | `96 XX XX XX` | `+227 96 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+22720201234` | `+227 20 20 12 34` | `20 20 12 34` | Niamey — 2 marks fixed service, the following digit the operator |
| mobile | `+22796051234` | `+227 96 05 12 34` | `96 05 12 34` | Mobile — the 96 prefix, allocated under the 2006 restructuring |

## Notes

- The first two digits (AB) identify the service or network; the remaining six identify the subscriber.
- No trunk prefix or geographic area codes.
