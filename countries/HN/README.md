# Honduras — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `HN` / `HND`  
**Country calling code:** +504  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXXX-XXXX
International: +504 XXXX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (2) | `2XXX-XXXX` | `+504 2XXX XXXX` |
| Mobile — Tigo (9), Digicel (8), Hondutel (7), Claro (3) | `9XXX-XXXX` | `+504 9XXX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+50422345678` | `+504 2234 5678` | `2234-5678` | Fixed line — the leading 2 was added in 2010 when numbers went from 7 to 8 digits |
| mobile | `+50491234567` | `+504 9123 4567` | `9123-4567` | Mobile — 9 denotes Tigo |

## Notes

- No area codes or trunk prefix; the leading digit encodes service and operator.
- Mobile numbers expanded from 7 to 8 digits in 2007 and fixed lines in 2010.
