# Norway — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `NO` / `NOR`  
**Country calling code:** +47  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX XX (landline) / XXX XX XXX (mobile)
International: +47 XX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line | `XX XX XX XX` | `+47 XX XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (4x / 9x) | `XXX XX XXX` | `+47 XXX XX XXX` |
| Freephone | `800 XX XXX` | `+47 800 XX XXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+4722123456` | `+47 22 12 34 56` | `22 12 34 56` | Historically Oslo; geographic meaning was abolished in January 2020 |
| mobile | `+4741234567` | `+47 412 34 567` | `412 34 567` | Mobile — ranges beginning 4 or 9 |
| toll-free | `+4780012345` | `+47 800 12 345` | `800 12 345` | Grønt nummer (freephone) |

## Notes

- Norway has no trunk prefix and no area codes: all subscriber numbers are 8 digits, dialled in full every time.
- Geographic significance of the leading digits was formally abolished in January 2020 — a number beginning 22 no longer implies Oslo.
- Landlines are conventionally grouped in four pairs; mobiles in a 3-2-3 pattern.
