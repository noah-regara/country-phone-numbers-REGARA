# Antarctica — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `AQ` / `ATA`  
**Country calling code:** +672, +64  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** variable

## Formatting

```
National:      1X XXXX (Australian Antarctic bases)
International: +672 1X XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Davis Station (10 XXXX) | `10 XXXX` | `+672 10 XXXX` |
| Mawson Station (11 XXXX) | `11 XXXX` | `+672 11 XXXX` |
| Casey Station (12 XXXX) | `12 XXXX` | `+672 12 XXXX` |
| Macquarie Island (13 XXXX) | `13 XXXX` | `+672 13 XXXX` |
| Vessels, Wilkins and mobiles (14 XXXX) | `14 XXXX` | `+672 14 XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+672101234` | `+672 10 1234` | `10 1234` | Davis Station — Australian Antarctic Division numbering |
| fixed-line | `+672121234` | `+672 12 1234` | `12 1234` | Casey Station |
| fixed-line | `+6449999999` | `+64 4 999 9999` | `04 999 9999` | Scott Base (NZ) and the nearby US McMurdo Station are reached through New Zealand's +64, not +672 |

## Notes

- Antarctica has no public telephone network. Calls reach research stations over satellite links, and each national programme numbers its own bases.
- +672 covers the Australian Antarctic Territory bases (Davis, Mawson, Casey) plus Macquarie Island — it is shared with Norfolk Island, which uses area code 3.
- New Zealand's Scott Base and the US McMurdo Station are reached via +64; other national programmes use their own home country codes or Iridium/Inmarsat satellite numbers (+870 range).
- The Scott Base example below follows New Zealand's numbering plan, including its trunk prefix 0 in the national form — the +672 base numbers have no trunk prefix.
- ISO 3166-1 assigns AQ to Antarctica as a whole, but no single numbering plan covers the continent.
