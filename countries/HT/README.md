# Haiti — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `HT` / `HTI`  
**Country calling code:** +509  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XXXX
International: +509 XX XX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (2X) | `22 XX XXXX` | `+509 22 XX XXXX` |
| Mobile (3X, 4X) | `30 XX XXXX` | `+509 30 XX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+50922123456` | `+509 22 12 3456` | `22 12 3456` | Port-au-Prince — NATCOM fixed line, 2X range |
| mobile | `+50930123456` | `+509 30 12 3456` | `30 12 3456` | Mobile — 3X is a Digicel Haiti range |

## Notes

- Haiti expanded from 7 to 8 digits on 2008-03-01.
- No trunk prefix or area codes; the leading digit distinguishes fixed (2) from mobile (3, 4).
