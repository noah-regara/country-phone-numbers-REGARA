# Burkina Faso — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `BF` / `BFA`  
**Country calling code:** +226  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX XX
International: +226 XX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line — Ouagadougou (50 30–50 38) | `50 XX XX XX` | `+226 50 XX XX XX` |
| Mobile — ONATEL (60, 61), Airtel (65, 66, 77), Telecel (68, 79) | `70 XX XX XX` | `+226 70 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+22650301234` | `+226 50 30 12 34` | `50 30 12 34` | Ouagadougou — the 50 30–50 38 fixed block |
| mobile | `+22670123456` | `+226 70 12 34 56` | `70 12 34 56` | Mobile — 7x range |

## Notes

- Burkina Faso restructured its plan in 2004, prefixing two digits to area codes to reach the current 8-digit format.
- No trunk prefix; numbers are written in pairs in the French style.
