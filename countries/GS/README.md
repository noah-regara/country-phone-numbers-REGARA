# South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `GS` / `SGS`  
**Country calling code:** +500  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 5 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXXXX (Falkland Islands plan)
International: +500 XXXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed / research station lines | `2XXXX` | `+500 2XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+50021234` | `+500 21234` | `21234` | South Georgia shares the Falkland Islands numbering plan; King Edward Point is served through it |

## Notes

- South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands have no separate country code and no permanent civilian population; the territory shares +500 with the Falkland Islands.
- Communications serve the British Antarctic Survey station at King Edward Point and visiting vessels, and satellite services are widely used in place of terrestrial numbering.
