# Chile — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `CL` / `CHL`  
**Country calling code:** +56  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `1XX0 (carrier-selected, e.g. 1230, 1880)`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      X XXXX XXXX (9 digits, no area codes)
International: +56 X XXXX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (2 = Santiago, other digits by region) | `2 XXXX XXXX` | `+56 2 XXXX XXXX` |
| Mobile (9) | `9 XXXX XXXX` | `+56 9 XXXX XXXX` |
| Toll-free | `800 XXX XXX` | `+56 800 XXX XXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+56221234567` | `+56 2 2123 4567` | `2 2123 4567` | Santiago — fixed lines renumbered onto the 2 prefix in 2012–2013 |
| mobile | `+56987654321` | `+56 9 8765 4321` | `9 8765 4321` | Mobile — the 9 prefix, though portability blurs the fixed/mobile distinction |
| toll-free | `+56800123456` | `+56 800 123 456` | `800 123 456` | Toll-free |

## Notes

- Chile has no geographic area codes and no trunk prefix — every call within Chile is a local call and all numbers are a flat 9 digits.
- Full number portability across fixed, mobile and VoIP means the leading digit no longer reliably identifies the line type.
- The international access prefix is carrier-selected in the form 1XX0.
