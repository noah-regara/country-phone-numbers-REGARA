# Denmark — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `DK` / `DNK`  
**Country calling code:** +45  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX XX
International: +45 XX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line | `XX XX XX XX` | `+45 XX XX XX XX` |
| Mobile | `XX XX XX XX` | `+45 XX XX XX XX` |
| Freephone | `80 XX XX XX` | `+45 80 XX XX XX` |
| Premium rate | `90 XX XX XX` | `+45 90 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+4533123456` | `+45 33 12 34 56` | `33 12 34 56` | Fixed line — 33 is a Copenhagen-origin range |
| mobile | `+4525876543` | `+45 25 87 65 43` | `25 87 65 43` | Mobile — 2x ranges |
| toll-free | `+4580123456` | `+45 80 12 34 56` | `80 12 34 56` | Freephone |

## Notes

- Denmark abolished area codes in 1989. All numbers are 8 digits with no trunk prefix, dialled in full for every call.
- Numbers are portable across both provider and geography, so the leading digits do not reliably indicate operator or location.
- Fixed and mobile numbers share the same 8-digit format and are distinguished only by allocated range.
