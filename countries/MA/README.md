# Morocco — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `MA` / `MAR`  
**Country calling code:** +212  
**Trunk prefix:** `0`  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      0AAA XX XX XX
International: +212 AAA XX XX XX (trunk 0 dropped)
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Casablanca (0522, 0529) | `0522 XX XX XX` | `+212 522 XX XX XX` |
| Rabat (0537), Marrakesh (0524), Fes (0535) | `0537 XX XX XX` | `+212 537 XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (06X, 07X) | `0661 XX XX XX` | `+212 661 XX XX XX` |
| Toll-free (080) | `0800 XX XX XX` | `+212 800 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+212522123456` | `+212 522 12 34 56` | `0522 12 34 56` | Casablanca — one of about ten prefixes serving the city |
| fixed-line | `+212537123456` | `+212 537 12 34 56` | `0537 12 34 56` | Rabat — prefix 0537 |
| mobile | `+212661123456` | `+212 661 12 34 56` | `0661 12 34 56` | Mobile — 06X and 07X ranges across Maroc Telecom, Orange Maroc and INWI |
| toll-free | `+212800123456` | `+212 800 12 34 56` | `0800 12 34 56` | Numéro vert (freephone) |

## Notes

- Closed 9-digit plan: the prefix must be dialled even for local calls.
- One geographic area may be served by several prefixes — Casablanca alone has around ten.
- Numbers grew from 6 digits (1990) to 7, then 8 (2000) and finally 9 (2009).
- Western Sahara is administered within the same +212 plan, principally under the 0528 prefix.
