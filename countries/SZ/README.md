# Eswatini — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `SZ` / `SWZ`  
**Country calling code:** +268  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XXXX
International: +268 XX XX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mbabane / Hhohho fixed (2404, 2405, 2406) | `24 XX XXXX` | `+268 24 XX XXXX` |
| Mobile — MTN Eswatini (76, 78, 79) | `79 XX XXXX` | `+268 79 XX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+26824041234` | `+268 24 04 1234` | `24 04 1234` | Mbabane — the 2404–2406 blocks serve the capital in the Hhohho region |
| mobile | `+26879123456` | `+268 79 12 3456` | `79 12 3456` | Mobile — non-geographic GSM on the 7x range |

## Notes

- Eswatini expanded to 8 digits in 2010–2011 by prepending 2 to fixed lines and 7 to mobile numbers.
- No trunk prefix or area codes.
