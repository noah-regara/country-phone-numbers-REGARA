# Panama — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `PA` / `PAN`  
**Country calling code:** +507  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 7 or 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXX-XXXX (fixed) / 6XXX-XXXX (mobile)
International: +507 XXX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line — Panama City (2, 3) | `2XX-XXXX` | `+507 2XX XXXX` |
| Mobile (6) | `6XXX-XXXX` | `+507 6XXX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+5072123456` | `+507 212 3456` | `212-3456` | Panama City — the leading 2 or 3 encodes location |
| mobile | `+50761234567` | `+507 6123 4567` | `6123-4567` | Mobile — 8-digit numbers beginning with 6, introduced August 2005 |

## Notes

- Panama has no area codes. Fixed lines are 7 digits with geography encoded in the first digit; mobile numbers are 8 digits beginning with 6.
- Numbers are recycled after a subscriber cancels or goes inactive.
