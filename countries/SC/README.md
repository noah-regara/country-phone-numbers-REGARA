# Seychelles — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `SC` / `SYC`  
**Country calling code:** +248  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 7 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      X XXX XXX
International: +248 X XXX XXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line — Cable & Wireless Seychelles (4XX XXXX) | `4 XXX XXX` | `+248 4 XXX XXX` |
| Mobile — Airtel, Cable & Wireless (2XX XXXX) | `2 XXX XXX` | `+248 2 XXX XXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+2484201234` | `+248 4 201 234` | `4 201 234` | Fixed line — the 4 prefix is Cable & Wireless Seychelles |
| mobile | `+2482795678` | `+248 2 795 678` | `2 795 678` | Mobile — the 2 prefix (Airtel Seychelles) |

## Notes

- The numbering plan was revised in May 2011, moving to the current 7-digit format.
- No trunk prefix or area codes; the leading digit identifies the service.
