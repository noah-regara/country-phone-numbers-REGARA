# Western Sahara — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `EH` / `ESH`  
**Country calling code:** +212  
**Trunk prefix:** `0`  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      0528 XX XX XX (Moroccan plan)
International: +212 528 XX XX XX (trunk 0 dropped)
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laayoune / Dakhla region (0528) | `0528 XX XX XX` | `+212 528 XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (06X, 07X — Moroccan ranges) | `0661 XX XX XX` | `+212 661 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+212528123456` | `+212 528 12 34 56` | `0528 12 34 56` | Laayoune — the 0528 prefix serves the southern provinces including Western Sahara |
| mobile | `+212661123456` | `+212 661 12 34 56` | `0661 12 34 56` | Mobile — Moroccan mobile ranges apply throughout the territory |

## Notes

- Western Sahara has no separate country code. The territory is administered by Morocco for telecommunications purposes and falls entirely within the +212 numbering plan.
- The 0528 prefix covers the southern provinces including Laayoune and Dakhla.
- The territory's political status is disputed; this entry reflects the numbering arrangement in operational use, not a position on sovereignty.
