# Sao Tome and Principe — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `ST` / `STP`  
**Country calling code:** +239  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 7 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXX XXXX
International: +239 XXX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (2XX XXXX) | `222 XXXX` | `+239 222 XXXX` |
| Mobile — CST, Unitel STP (9XX XXXX) | `98X XXXX` | `+239 98X XXXX` |
| Freephone (8XX XXXX) | `8XX XXXX` | `+239 8XX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+2392220050` | `+239 222 0050` | `222 0050` | Santo Amaro area — fixed numbers begin with 2 |
| mobile | `+2399812345` | `+239 981 2345` | `981 2345` | Mobile — the 9 prefix denotes GSM service |

## Notes

- Closed 7-digit plan with no area codes or trunk prefix.
- The leading digit classifies the service: 2 fixed, 6 premium, 7 VPN, 8 freephone, 9 mobile.
