# Cameroon — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `CM` / `CMR`  
**Country calling code:** +237  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXX XX XX XX
International: +237 XXX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (2XX) | `2XX XX XX XX` | `+237 2XX XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (6XX) | `6XX XX XX XX` | `+237 6XX XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+237266401234` | `+237 266 40 12 34` | `266 40 12 34` | CAMTEL fixed line — all fixed numbers begin with 2 |
| mobile | `+237677123456` | `+237 677 12 34 56` | `677 12 34 56` | Mobile — all mobile numbers begin with 6 (MTN Cameroon, Orange Cameroun) |
| mobile | `+237690123456` | `+237 690 12 34 56` | `690 12 34 56` | Mobile — 69X range |

## Notes

- Cameroon expanded from 8 to 9 digits on 2014-11-21 by prefixing 2 (fixed) or 6 (mobile), raising capacity from 80 to 800 million numbers.
- There is no trunk prefix: the same 9 digits are dialled domestically and internationally after +237.
