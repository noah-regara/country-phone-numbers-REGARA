# Greenland — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `GL` / `GRL`  
**Country calling code:** +299  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 6 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX
International: +299 XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line, Nuuk (3x) | `3X XX XX` | `+299 3X XX XX` |
| Fixed line, other settlements (6x–9x) | `8X XX XX` | `+299 8X XX XX` |
| Mobile (21–29, 42–49, 51–59) | `2X XX XX` | `+299 2X XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+299321234` | `+299 32 12 34` | `32 12 34` | Nuuk — 31/32 serve the capital in Sermersooq |
| fixed-line | `+299851234` | `+299 85 12 34` | `85 12 34` | Sisimiut, West Greenland |
| mobile | `+299221234` | `+299 22 12 34` | `22 12 34` | Mobile — 21–29 range |

## Notes

- Greenland is part of the Kingdom of Denmark but has its own country code +299.
- All numbers are 6 digits, written as three pairs, with no trunk prefix.
- The first two digits still carry rough geographic meaning for fixed lines.
