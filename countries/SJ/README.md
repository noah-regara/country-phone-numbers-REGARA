# Svalbard and Jan Mayen — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `SJ` / `SJM`  
**Country calling code:** +47  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX XX (Norwegian plan)
International: +47 XX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (Longyearbyen, historically 79 02) | `79 XX XX XX` | `+47 79 XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (Norwegian ranges) | `XXX XX XXX` | `+47 XXX XX XXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+4779021234` | `+47 79 02 12 34` | `79 02 12 34` | Longyearbyen, Svalbard — the 79 02 block historically served the archipelago |
| mobile | `+4741234567` | `+47 412 34 567` | `412 34 567` | Mobile — ordinary Norwegian mobile range |

## Notes

- Svalbard and Jan Mayen have no separate country code; they use Norway's +47 and its 8-digit closed plan.
- Norway abolished geographic significance of number ranges in January 2020, so the 79 block no longer formally denotes Svalbard.
