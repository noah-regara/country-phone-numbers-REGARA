# Benin — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `BJ` / `BEN`  
**Country calling code:** +229  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 10 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      01 YY XX XXXX
International: +229 01 YY XX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line — Littoral/Atlantique incl. Cotonou (0121) | `01 21 XX XXXX` | `+229 01 21 XX XXXX` |
| Mobile — MTN (0150–0159), Moov (0160–0169) | `01 50 XX XXXX` | `+229 01 50 XX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+2290121301234` | `+229 01 21 30 1234` | `01 21 30 1234` | Cotonou — 21 is the Littoral/Atlantique code, 30 the Cadjehoun district |
| mobile | `+2290150123456` | `+229 01 50 12 3456` | `01 50 12 3456` | Mobile — the 50–59 block is MTN Benin |

## Notes

- On 2024-11-30 Benin prefixed '01' to every number, taking the NSN from 8 to 10 digits. Older 8-digit Beninese numbers are no longer valid.
- The leading 01 is part of the number, not a trunk prefix — it is dialled from abroad too.
