# Saint Barthélemy — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `BL` / `BLM`  
**Country calling code:** +590  
**Trunk prefix:** `0`  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      0590 XX XX XX
International: +590 590 XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (0590 27…) | `0590 27 XX XX` | `+590 590 27 XX XX` |
| Mobile (0690) | `0690 XX XX XX` | `+590 690 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+590590271234` | `+590 590 27 12 34` | `0590 27 12 34` | Saint Barthélemy — the 27 block within the shared Guadeloupe 0590 range |
| mobile | `+590690123456` | `+590 690 12 34 56` | `0690 12 34 56` | Mobile — shares Guadeloupe's 0690 range |

## Notes

- Saint Barthélemy has no separate country code; it shares +590 with Guadeloupe and Saint Martin.
- The three territories are distinguished by subscriber block, not by prefix, so the country cannot be determined from the number alone.
