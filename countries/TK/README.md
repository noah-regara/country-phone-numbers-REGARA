# Tokelau — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `TK` / `TKL`  
**Country calling code:** +690  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 5 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XXXXX
International: +690 XXXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed and mobile services | `2XXXX` | `+690 2XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+69021234` | `+690 21234` | `21234` | Tokelau — 5-digit number after the November 2015 expansion |

## Notes

- Tokelau moved from 4-digit to 5-digit numbers in November 2015 by prefixing '2' to existing numbers.
- The registered plan permits 3 to 7 digits, but numbers in service are 5 digits.
- Tokelau is a New Zealand territory but holds its own country code +690.
