# Malawi — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `MW` / `MWI`  
**Country calling code:** +265  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 7 or 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      X XXX XXX (7-digit) / X XXXX XXXX (9-digit)
International: +265 X XXX XXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographic — Malawi Telecom (1) | `1 XXX XXX` | `+265 1 XXX XXX` |
| Mobile — TNM (8), Airtel (9) | `9 XXXX XXXX` | `+265 9 XXXX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+2651234567` | `+265 1 234 567` | `1 234 567` | Lilongwe — Malawi Telecom geographic numbers on the 1 prefix are 7 digits |
| mobile | `+265981234567` | `+265 9 8123 4567` | `9 8123 4567` | Mobile — 9 is an Airtel Malawi range; mobile NSNs are 9 digits |

## Notes

- Malawi has no area codes; the leading digit identifies the operator and service.
- Legacy 7-digit geographic numbers on the 1 prefix coexist with 9-digit numbers introduced after the 2009 plan revision, so length varies.
