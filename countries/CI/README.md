# Côte d'Ivoire — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `CI` / `CIV`  
**Country calling code:** +225  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 10 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XXX XXX
International: +225 XX XX XXX XXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (27) | `27 XX XX XX XX` | `+225 27 XX XX XX XX` |
| Mobile — Moov/Atlantic (01), MTN (05), Orange (07) | `07 XX XX XX XX` | `+225 07 XX XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+2252721123456` | `+225 27 21 12 34 56` | `27 21 12 34 56` | Abidjan — 27 is the fixed-line prefix, 21 the Abidjan area code |
| mobile | `+2250712345678` | `+225 07 12 34 56 78` | `07 12 34 56 78` | Mobile — 07 is an Orange range |
| mobile | `+2250512345678` | `+225 05 12 34 56 78` | `05 12 34 56 78` | Mobile — 05 is an MTN range |

## Notes

- Côte d'Ivoire renumbered on 2021-01-31, going from 8 to 10 digits by prepending two digits that encode service and carrier (27 fixed, 01/05/07 mobile).
- An earlier expansion from 6 to 8 digits took place on 2000-01-15.
- There is no trunk prefix — the full 10 digits are dialled for every domestic call.
