# Saint Martin (French part) — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `MF` / `MAF`  
**Country calling code:** +590  
**Trunk prefix:** `0`  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      0590 XX XX XX
International: +590 590 XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (0590 87…) | `0590 87 XX XX` | `+590 590 87 XX XX` |
| Mobile (0690) | `0690 XX XX XX` | `+590 690 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+590590871234` | `+590 590 87 12 34` | `0590 87 12 34` | Saint Martin (French part) — the 87 block within the shared 0590 range |
| mobile | `+590690123456` | `+590 690 12 34 56` | `0690 12 34 56` | Mobile — shares Guadeloupe's 0690 range |

## Notes

- Saint Martin (French part) shares +590 with Guadeloupe and Saint Barthélemy.
- The Dutch half of the same island, Sint Maarten, is entirely separate: it sits in the NANP as +1 721.
