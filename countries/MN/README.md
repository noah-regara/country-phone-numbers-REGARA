# Mongolia — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `MN` / `MNG`  
**Country calling code:** +976  
**Trunk prefix:** `01 (carrier-based; 02 for the railway network)`  
**International call prefix:** `001 (also 002, 003, 008, 009)`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XXXX
International: +976 XX XX XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ulaanbaatar (11) | `11 XX XXXX` | `+976 11 XX XXXX` |
| Provincial (2-digit area codes) | `XX XX XXXX` | `+976 XX XX XXXX` |
| Mobile — Mobicom (94, 95, 99, 85), Unitel (80, 86, 88, 89), Skytel (90–92, 96), G-Mobile (93, 97, 98) | `94 XX XXXX` | `+976 94 XX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+97611452222` | `+976 11 45 2222` | `11 45 2222` | Ulaanbaatar — area code 11 (Mongolia Telecom) |
| mobile | `+97694123456` | `+976 94 12 3456` | `94 12 3456` | Mobile — 94/95 are Mobicom ranges |
| mobile | `+97688123456` | `+976 88 12 3456` | `88 12 3456` | Mobile — 88/89 are Unitel ranges |

## Notes

- Mongolia's domestic trunk access is carrier-based rather than a single national 0: 01 reaches the Mongolia Telecom network and 02 the railway network.
- The international access prefix likewise varies by carrier (001, 002, 003, 008, 009).
- All NSNs are 8 digits. Mobile numbers begin with 6, 8 or 9.
