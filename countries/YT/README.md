# Mayotte — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `YT` / `MYT`  
**Country calling code:** +262  
**Trunk prefix:** `0`  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 9 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      0269 XX XX XX
International: +262 269 XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (0269) | `0269 XX XX XX` | `+262 269 XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (0639) | `0639 XX XX XX` | `+262 639 XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+262269123456` | `+262 269 12 34 56` | `0269 12 34 56` | Mayotte fixed — the 0269 range distinguishes Mayotte from Réunion inside +262 |
| mobile | `+262639123456` | `+262 639 12 34 56` | `0639 12 34 56` | Mobile — the 0639 range |

## Notes

- Mayotte has no country code of its own: it shares +262 with Réunion and is identified by the internal prefixes 269 (fixed) and 639 (mobile).
- Mayotte moved from +269 (now Comoros only) to +262 in 2007.
