# Belize — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `BZ` / `BLZ`  
**Country calling code:** +501  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 7 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      ZNY-XXXX
International: +501 ZNY XXXX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Belize District (2), Cayo (8), Orange Walk (3), Corozal (4), Stann Creek (5), Toledo (7) | `22X-XXXX` | `+501 22X XXXX` |
| Mobile, countrywide (6) | `6XX-XXXX` | `+501 6XX XXXX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+5012224940` | `+501 222 4940` | `222-4940` | Belize City — 2 = Belize District, second 2 = BTL's PSTN service |
| mobile | `+5016149076` | `+501 614 9076` | `614-9076` | Mobile — 6 is countrywide mobile, 1 identifies the provider |

## Notes

- Since 2002 numbers follow a ZNY-XXXX structure: Z is the geographic/service code, N the service type (0 prepaid, 1 mobile, 2 PSTN), and the rest the subscriber number.
- The full 7 digits are dialled for every local call regardless of distance.
