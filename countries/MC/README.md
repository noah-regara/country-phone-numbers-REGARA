# Monaco — Phone Number Formats

**ISO 3166-1:** `MC` / `MCO`  
**Country calling code:** +377  
**Trunk prefix:** none  
**International call prefix:** `00`  
**National significant number:** 8 digits

## Formatting

```
National:      XX XX XX XX
International: +377 XX XX XX XX
```

## Number ranges

| Range | National format | International format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed line (9x) | `9X XX XX XX` | `+377 9X XX XX XX` |
| Mobile (4x, 6x) | `6X XX XX XX` | `+377 6X XX XX XX` |

## Examples

| Type | E.164 | International | National | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fixed-line | `+37793151234` | `+377 93 15 12 34` | `93 15 12 34` | Fixed line — Monaco landlines begin with 9 |
| mobile | `+37764123456` | `+377 64 12 34 56` | `64 12 34 56` | Mobile — 4x and 6x ranges |

## Notes

- Monaco left the French numbering plan on 1996-06-21 and took country code +377. Calls between Monaco and neighbouring France must be dialled in full international format.
- Numbers are 8 digits, written as four pairs in the French style, with no trunk prefix.
